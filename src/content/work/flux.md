## Overview

Hierarchical time series forecasting system for retail inventory planning. Combines multiple forecasting approaches with optimal reconciliation to produce coherent forecasts at all aggregation levels.

## Methods

Implements ETS, ARIMA, and Prophet as base forecasters with MinT optimal reconciliation. Cross-validation with rolling origin for honest performance evaluation.

## Impact

- 18% MAPE reduction vs. existing system
- Coherent forecasts across 500+ SKUs
- Estimated $2M annual savings in carrying costs
