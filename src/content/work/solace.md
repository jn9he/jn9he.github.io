## Overview

An accessible data visualization system for communicating public health trends to general audiences. Built with D3.js and designed to work across devices with screen reader support.

## Design Principles

- Clarity over decoration
- Color-blind safe palettes
- Progressive disclosure of complexity
- Mobile-first responsive charts

## Outcomes

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant
- Used by 3 county health departments
- Featured in local news coverage of health trends
