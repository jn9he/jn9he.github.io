## Overview

A quasi-experimental study estimating the causal effect of remote learning modalities on undergraduate performance outcomes using difference-in-differences and synthetic control methods.

## Methodology

Leveraged the natural experiment of COVID-19 policy changes across universities. Used staggered diff-in-diff with Callaway-Sant'Anna estimators to handle heterogeneous treatment timing.

## Findings

- Average treatment effect of -0.15 GPA points for fully remote
- Heterogeneous effects by discipline (STEM most affected)
- No significant long-term persistence after return to in-person
