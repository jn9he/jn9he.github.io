## Overview

A complete redesign of an academic course dashboard focusing on data visualization principles to surface actionable insights for students and instructors.

## Design Process

Started with user research interviews, mapped pain points, and iterated through low-fi wireframes before building a high-fidelity prototype in Figma. Conducted usability testing with 12 participants.

## Outcomes

- 30% increase in daily active usage
- 85% task completion rate (up from 62%)
- Adopted by 3 departments for the following semester
