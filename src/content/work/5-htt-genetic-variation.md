## Overview

An academic research paper investigating how genetic vulnerability (the 5-HTT serotonin transporter polymorphism) interacts with environmental stressors to affect depression risk.

## Tech Stack

R (Tidyverse, ggplot2), LaTeX.

## Approach

- Cleaned and standardized 24 clinical and behavioral variables using R (Tidyverse).
- Formulated a multivariable regression model with second-order interaction terms between stressful life events (SLE) and genotype alleles.
- Ran residual diagnostics and heteroscedasticity checks to validate the model.
- Typeset the full paper in LaTeX, including regression tables, residual plots, and statistical graphics built with ggplot2.

## Outcome

Published academic paper evaluating how genotype and stress exposure jointly shift the probability of depression onset.
