## Overview

An NLP pipeline that processes thousands of customer feedback entries daily, classifying sentiment and extracting actionable themes for product teams.

## Technical Stack

Built with Python, Hugging Face transformers, and deployed on AWS Lambda for serverless scaling. Uses a fine-tuned DistilBERT model with domain-specific vocabulary.

## Results

- 94% sentiment classification accuracy
- Processes 10,000+ entries per hour
- Reduced manual review time by 70%
